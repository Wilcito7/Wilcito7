## `04 / FEATURED PROJECTS`

<table>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ◈ MakeIt

**AI-powered workshop optimization**

A concept focused on solving one of the most underestimated problems inside fabrication environments:

> **Where is the tool, material or resource I need?**

MakeIt explores intelligent inventory, tool tracking and operational optimization for:

- Fabrication workshops
- Makerspaces
- Robotics clubs
- Repair environments

`AI` `Optimization` `Inventory` `Workshops`

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ◈ TerraSense

**Environmental & disaster monitoring**

A distributed monitoring concept designed to detect environmental conditions through a network of sensor nodes.

The system combines:

**Sensors → LoRa → Central Server → Alerts**

Monitoring variables such as:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Smoke
- Water levels

`IoT` `LoRa` `Sensors` `Emergency Systems`

</td>
